# Employee Handbook 2024

## Welcome
Welcome to OmniSift! This handbook outlines our policies, benefits, and expectations. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Please read carefully and reach out to HR with any questions.

## 1. Employment Policies

### 1.1 Equal Employment Opportunity
OmniSift is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

### 1.2 At-Will Employment
Employment at OmniSift is at-will. Either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice.

### 1.3 Remote Work Policy
**Effective January 2024:**
- All employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**
- Team leads must approve schedules in advance
- **Core hours: 10am-3pm EST** (mandatory availability)
- Remote work stipend: **$500 annual home office stipend**
- One-time **$300 equipment allowance** for new hires
- Ergonomic assessment available upon request
- Must maintain secure workspace and reliable internet

### 1.4 Work Hours and Overtime
- Standard work week: 40 hours
- Non-exempt employees: overtime paid at 1.5x for hours >40/week
- Exempt employees: no overtime, expected to manage workload
- Time tracking required for all non-exempt employees

## 2. Compensation and Benefits

### 2.1 Health Insurance
Three tiers available, **Company covers 80% of premiums**:
- **Bronze**: High deductible, HSA eligible, lowest employee cost
- **Silver**: Balanced deductible/co-pay, popular choice
- **Gold**: Low deductible, comprehensive coverage, highest employee cost

**Open enrollment: November 1-15 annually.** Qualifying life events allow mid-year changes.

### 2.2 401(k) Retirement Plan
- **100% match on first 4% of salary**
- **Vesting: Immediate**
- Roth and traditional options available
- Automatic enrollment at 3% (opt-out available)
- Investment options: Target-date funds, index funds, ESG options

### 2.3 Parental Leave
- **Primary caregiver: 12 weeks paid at 100%**
- **Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid at 100%**
- Additional **4 weeks unpaid** available (job protected)
- Applies to birth, adoption, foster care
- Must be taken within 12 months of qualifying event

### 2.4 PTO (Paid Time Off)
- **Years 1-3: 15 days annual accrual** (1.25 days/month)
- **Year 4+: 20 days annual accrual** (1.67 days/month)
- **10 company holidays** (fixed schedule published annually)
- **Unlimited sick days** with manager approval for >3 consecutive days
- PTO rolls over up to 10 days (use-it-or-lose-it for excess)
- Minimum 2 weeks notice for vacations >5 days

### 2.5 Additional Benefits
- **Learning stipend**: $2,000/year for courses, certifications, conferences
- **Wellness stipend**: $500/year (gym, apps, equipment)
- **Commuter benefits**: Pre-tax up to IRS limit
- **Life insurance**: 2x salary (company paid), supplemental available
- **Disability**: Short-term (60% salary, 90 days), Long-term (60% salary, to age 65)

## 3. Performance and Development

### 3.1 Review Cycles
- **Quarterly check-ins** with managers (informal, 30 min)
- **Formal reviews semi-annually** (June and December)
- **Rating scale:**
  - **Exceeds**: Consistently exceeds expectations, drives innovation
  - **Meets**: Solid performer, delivers quality work reliably
  - **Developing**: Shows promise, needs focused growth in areas
  - **Needs Improvement**: Performance below expectations, PIP possible

### 3.2 Career Development
- Individual Development Plans (IDPs) created annually
- Internal mobility encouraged after 12 months in role
- Mentorship program: opt-in matching
- Leadership track for senior ICs and managers

### 3.3 Compensation Reviews
- Annual salary reviews tied to December review cycle
- Market adjustments based on role/location benchmarks
- Equity refresh grants for top performers
- Promotions effective first of following quarter

## 4. Code of Conduct

### 4.1 Professional Standards
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Communicate openly and honestly
- Protect confidential information
- Avoid conflicts of interest

### 4.2 Anti-Harassment
Zero tolerance for harassment of any kind. Report to HR, manager, or anonymous hotline (1-800-XXX-XXXX). All reports investigated promptly.

### 4.3 Data Security
- Annual security awareness training required
- Phishing simulations conducted quarterly
- Report suspicious emails to security@company.com
- No unauthorized software or cloud services
- Encrypt all devices, use password manager

### 4.4 Expense Reimbursement
- Submit within **30 days** with receipts
- **Per diem rates**: $75/day domestic, $120/day international
- Business meals: $50/day limit (itemized receipt required)
- Travel: Book through corporate travel portal
- Personal expenses not reimbursable

## 5. Leave Policies

### 5.1 Medical Leave
- FMLA: 12 weeks job-protected (eligible after 12 months)
- Short-term disability integration
- Doctor certification required for >3 days

### 5.2 Bereavement Leave
- 5 days for immediate family
- 3 days for extended family
- Additional PTO may be used

### 5.3 Jury Duty / Civic Duty
- Paid time off for jury duty (up to 10 days)
- Voting time: 2 hours paid
- Military leave: Per USERRA requirements

## 6. Separation

### 6.1 Voluntary Resignation
- 2 weeks notice standard (4 weeks for senior roles)
- Exit interview with HR
- Final paycheck on next regular pay date
- COBRA information provided

### 6.2 Involuntary Termination
- Severance: 2 weeks per year of service (minimum 4, maximum 26 weeks)
- COBRA subsidy for 3 months
- Outplacement services provided
- Return of equipment within 7 days

### 6.3 Return of Property
All company property (laptop, phone, badge, credit card) must be returned on or before last day.
