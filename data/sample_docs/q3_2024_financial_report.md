## Revenue Recognition Policy (ASC 606)
Under ASC 606, revenue is recognized when control of goods or services transfers to the customer. For subscription contracts, revenue is recognized ratably over the contract term. Implementation fees are recognized upon completion of onboarding milestones.

**Five-Step Model Application:**
1. **Identify the contract**: SaaS agreements with enterprise customers
2. **Identify performance obligations**: Platform access, implementation services, support
3. **Determine transaction price**: Fixed fees + variable usage-based components
4. **Allocate transaction price**: Standalone selling price basis
5. **Recognize revenue**: Over time for subscriptions, point-in-time for implementation

2. Legal notification within 24 hours per GDPR Article 33
