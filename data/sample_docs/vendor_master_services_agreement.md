# Master Services Agreement (MSA) - Vendor Template

## Agreement Overview
This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between OmniSift Inc. ("Company") and the vendor ("Vendor") effective upon signature. This Agreement governs all Statements of Work (SOWs) executed hereunder.

## 1. Services and Deliverables
Vendor shall perform the services described in each mutually agreed Statement of Work. Each SOW shall specify:
- Scope of services
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Timeline and milestones
- Fees and payment schedule
- Resource commitments

## 2. Fees and Payment
- Invoices submitted monthly in arrears
- Net 30 payment terms
- Late payments bear interest at 1.5% per month
- Expenses pre-approved in writing, reimbursed at cost

## 3. Intellectual Property
### 3.1 Company IP
All work product, deliverables, and inventions created under this Agreement are "work made for hire" owned exclusively by Company. Vendor assigns all rights, title, and interest.

### 3.2 Vendor IP
Vendor retains ownership of pre-existing IP. Vendor grants Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use Vendor IP incorporated in deliverables.

## 4. Confidentiality and NDA Terms
### 4.1 Definition
"Confidential Information" includes all non-public information disclosed by either party, including: trade secrets, customer data, financial information, technical data, business plans.

### 4.2 Obligations
- Maintain confidentiality for 3 years post-termination
- Disclose only to personnel with need-to-know
- Personnel bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations
- Notify disclosing party within 24 hours of any unauthorized disclosure

### 4.3 Exceptions
Information that is: publicly known, independently developed, rightfully received from third party, or required by law (with notice).

## 5. Indemnification and Liability Caps

### 5.1 Vendor Indemnification
Vendor shall indemnify Company against claims arising from:
- IP infringement by Vendor deliverables
- Breach of confidentiality obligations
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Violation of applicable laws

### 5.2 Liability Caps
**Total aggregate liability capped at fees paid in preceding 12 months.**

**Exceptions to cap (uncapped liability):**
- Breach of confidentiality (Section 4)
- IP infringement (Section 3)
- Gross negligence or willful misconduct
- Data protection violations

**Service Level Credits (not subject to cap):**
- 99.9% uptime SLA
- 10% credit for <99.9% uptime
- 25% credit for <99.5% uptime
- 50% credit for <99.0% uptime
- Measured monthly per SOW

## 6. Term and Termination

### 6.1 Term
Initial term: 2 years from effective date. Auto-renews for successive 1-year terms unless terminated.

### 6.2 Termination for Convenience
Either party may terminate for convenience with **60 days written notice**.
- Customer pays for services rendered through termination date
- Plus any non-cancellable third-party commitments
- Transition assistance for 30 days post-termination

### 6.3 Termination for Cause
Immediate termination for material breach uncured after 30 days notice.

## 7. Force Majeure
Neither party liable for delays due to events beyond reasonable control:
- Natural disasters, war, terrorism
- Pandemics, epidemics
- Government actions, regulations
- Internet infrastructure failures

Affected party must notify within 5 days and use reasonable efforts to mitigate.

## 8. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
**Governing Law:** Delaware law (without conflict of laws principles).

**Exclusive Jurisdiction:** Delaware state and federal courts.

**Dispute Resolution:** Mandatory mediation before litigation. Each party bears own costs.

## 9. Data Protection
Vendor shall:
- Process personal data only per Company instructions
- Implement appropriate technical/organizational measures
- Notify Company within 24 hours of data breach
- Assist with DPIAs and data subject requests
- Delete/return data upon termination

## 10. Insurance
Vendor shall maintain:
- Commercial General Liability: $2M
- Professional Liability (E&O): $5M
- Cyber Liability: $5M
- Workers' Compensation: Statutory

Company named as additional insured where applicable.

## 11. General Provisions
- Entire agreement, supersedes prior agreements
- Amendments in writing signed by both parties
- No assignment without consent (except affiliates)
- Severability: invalid provisions severed
- Waiver must be in writing
- Notices via email to designated contacts
